# Data and code availability

## Submission statement

The analysis code, configuration files, aggregate numerical results, provenance manifests, and scripts required to reproduce the reported summary statistics are openly available in Zenodo at [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23059738](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23059738). The QuickBird-2, WorldView-2, and Pléiades-1A imagery, image-derived training and reference data, patches, and coordinate-bearing geospatial products are not redistributed because of third-party licensing and data-governance restrictions. The commercial imagery may be obtained from the respective providers subject to their licensing terms. This repository documents the excluded inputs and the requirements for a licensed end-to-end rerun.
